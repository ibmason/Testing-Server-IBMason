Private Equity Placement Architecture

Our curriculum is engineered to transform sell-side analysts and high-achieving undergraduates into elite buyside candidates, mastering the exact frameworks top-tier megafunds and upper-middle market funds evaluate.

LBO Modeling Mastery

Move beyond basic templates. Build and stress-test institutional grade Leveraged Buyout models under intense time constraints.

  • 3-Statement LBOs: Build dynamic debt schedules, revolvers, waterfalls, and PIK interest modules from scratch.
  • Paper LBO Execution: Master the mental math shortcuts to pass rapid-fire on-cycle oral LBO tests in under 5 minutes.
  • Complex Mechanics: Practice dividend recaps, add-on acquisitions, and management rollover structures.

Investor Case & CIM Analysis

Develop the "Investor Mindset." Learn how to quickly dissect a Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) and defend an investment thesis.

  • Investment Memo Writing: Learn how to draft bulletproof investment theses, identifying key business risks and mitigants.
  • Megafund Case Studies: Deconstruct real-world case templates used by Apollo, Blackstone, and KKR.
  • Verbal Defenses: Work 1-on-1 with coaches to pitch your long/short stock ideas and deal history under pressure.

Headhunter & On-Cycle Strategy

Navigate the chaotic, highly-accelerated on-cycle recruiting window with a precise game plan and immediate headhunter access.

  • Headhunter Optimization: Master your positioning for top firms like CPI, Henkel Search Partners, and Amity.
  • Resume Architecture: Craft an "Irresistible Buyside Resume" optimized for immediate automated parsing.
  • Off-Cycle Positioning: Playbooks targeting middle-market and growth equity shops bypassing the on-cycle rush.
