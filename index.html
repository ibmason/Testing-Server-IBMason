SIMPLE 4 STEP PROCESS

How Our Coaching Works

No prerecorded video libraries or bloated courses. Just direct, one on one coaching built around your recruiting timeline, from your first call to your first day on the desk.

Step 01

Strategy Call

We start with an honest look at your background, school, target banks, technical level, and recruiting timeline, then build your plan around it.

Step 02

Resume and Story

We rework your resume, LinkedIn, and "tell me about yourself" answer so screening software and bankers see your strongest experience.

Step 03

Networking and Interview Prep

You work directly with a current or former banker on cold outreach, coffee chats, technical questions, and mock interviews, with honest feedback after every session.

Step 04

Superdays, Offers and Pre Desk

We prepare you for Superdays, coach you through your offer and compensation negotiation, and get you ready for day one with pre desk training.
